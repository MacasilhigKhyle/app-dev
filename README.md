# 💗 My Favorite Anime: 3D Kanojo: Real Girl

> "Sometimes, love comes into your life when you least expect it."

## 📺 Introduction

**3D Kanojo: Real Girl** (*3D彼女 リアルガール*) is a Japanese romantic comedy and slice-of-life anime based on the manga written by **Mao Nanami**. The anime was produced by *Hoods Entertainment* and aired from 2018 to 2019.

I like this anime because it shows how **love, friendship, and understanding can change a person's life**. It combines funny moments, emotional scenes, and realistic relationship problems that make the story memorable.

---

## 📖 Story Synopsis

The story follows **Hikari Tsutsui**, a socially awkward high school student who prefers spending his time playing video games and watching anime rather than interacting with people in real life.

One day, he meets **Iroha Igarashi**, a beautiful and confident girl who seems to be his complete opposite. Unexpectedly, Iroha becomes his girlfriend, and Hikari begins to experience the joys and challenges of a real relationship.

As their relationship develops, Hikari learns to open up to others, face his insecurities, and understand what it truly means to love someone.

*The anime is more than just a love story; it is also about personal growth, acceptance, and the importance of genuine connections.*

| Character | Description |

## 🎬 Season Guide

The series has two seasons, with 12 episodes in each season.

- [x] **Season 1 (2018)** — Introduces Hikari and Iroha and follows the beginning of their relationship.
- [x] **Season 2 (2019)** — Continues their love story as they face more serious challenges and important decisions.

For the full experience, I recommend watching **Season 1 before Season 2** so that the character development and emotional moments make sense.

## 💕 Why I Like This Anime

There are several reasons why *3D Kanojo: Real Girl* is one of my favorite anime series:

1. **A heartwarming romance** — Hikari and Iroha's relationship develops as they learn to understand and support each other.
2. **Relatable characters** — Hikari's struggles with confidence and social interactions make his growth meaningful.
3. **A balance of comedy and drama** — The funny scenes make the story enjoyable, while the emotional moments give it depth.
4. **Meaningful life lessons** — The series explores acceptance, loyalty, trust, and the courage to express one's feelings.
5. **Memorable character development** — The characters grow through their friendships, mistakes, and experiences.

## 💭 My Favorite Lesson from the Anime

> Love is not about being perfect. It is about accepting each other's flaws, learning together, and choosing to support one another.

This is one of the ideas I appreciate about the series. Hikari and Iroha show that two people with very different personalities can still build a meaningful relationship.

## ✅ Final Thoughts

Overall, **3D Kanojo: Real Girl** is a heartwarming anime that combines romance, comedy, drama, and personal growth. Its story reminds me that real relationships require honesty, trust, patience, and understanding.

I would recommend this anime to people who enjoy **romantic anime with emotional storytelling, lovable characters, and a satisfying character journey**.

**My rating:** 9/10 💖
